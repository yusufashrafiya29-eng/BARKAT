## 🏢 BARKAT Pvt Ltd — Complete Step-by-Step Process with Charges

---

### **PHASE 1 — Pre-Registration (1–2 din)**

---

#### 📌 Step 1: DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) Banwao
> Har director ka apna DSC hona chahiye — yeh online documents sign karne ke liye use hota hai

- **Kahan se:** eMudhra, Sify, NCode (authorized DSC providers)
- **Kya chahiye:** Aadhaar, PAN, Photo, Mobile Number
- **Time:** 1–2 din
- **Cost:**

| Type | Price |
|------|-------|
| Class 3 DSC (2 year) | ₹1,500 – ₹2,000 per director |
| Agar 2 directors ho | ₹3,000 – ₹4,000 total |

---

#### 📌 Step 2: Company Name Decide + Check Karo
> "BARKAT Technologies Pvt Ltd" ya "BARKAT Solutions Pvt Ltd" — naam unique hona chahiye

- **Kahan check karo:** [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in) → MCA21 portal pe free name search
- **Rule:** Naam already registered nahi hona chahiye
- **Cost:** ₹0 (free check)
- **Tip:** 2–3 alternatives ready rakho (BARKAT Tech, BARKAT Digital, etc.)

---

### **PHASE 2 — Registration on MCA Portal (3–5 din)**

---

#### 📌 Step 3: SPICe+ Form Fill Karo (Main Registration Form)
> Government ka single form hai jisme company registration, PAN, TAN, GST sab ek saath hota hai

**Is form mein yeh hota hai:**
- Company name approval (Part A)
- Director details — DIN number milega automatically
- Registered office address
- MOA (Memorandum of Association) — company ka purpose
- AOA (Articles of Association) — internal rules

**Documents needed:**
```
✅ Aadhaar Card (all directors)
✅ PAN Card (all directors)
✅ Passport size photos
✅ Proof of registered office (rent agreement ya electricity bill)
✅ NOC from property owner (agar rented office hai)
```

- **Kahan file karo:** [mca.gov.in](https://www.mca.gov.in) → MCA21 V3 portal
- **Government Fee (Stamp Duty):**

| Share Capital | Govt Fee |
|--------------|----------|
| ₹1 Lakh capital | ~₹2,000 |
| ₹5 Lakh capital | ~₹4,000 |
| *(State-wise thoda vary karta hai)* | |

---

#### 📌 Step 4: COI (Certificate of Incorporation) Milega
> Yeh tumhari company ka "birth certificate" hai

- **Time:** 3–7 working days after form submission
- **Kya milega:**
  - Certificate of Incorporation (COI)
  - CIN Number (Corporate Identification Number)
  - PAN Number (auto-generated)
  - TAN Number (auto-generated)
- **Cost:** ₹0 (included in SPICe+ fee)

---

### **PHASE 3 — Post-Registration (1–2 hafte)**

---

#### 📌 Step 5: Bank Account Kholo
> Company ke naam pe current account — personal account use mat karo

- **Kahan:** HDFC, ICICI, Kotak (startup-friendly banks)
- **Documents:** COI + MOA + AOA + Directors' KYC + Board Resolution
- **Cost:** ₹0 – ₹10,000 (minimum balance depend karta hai bank pe)

| Bank | Min Balance | Recommendation |
|------|------------|----------------|
| ICICI iStartup | ₹0 | ✅ Best for startups |
| HDFC SmartUp | ₹10,000 | ✅ Good features |
| Kotak Startup | ₹0 | ✅ Easy online |

---

#### 📌 Step 6: GST Registration Karo ⚠️ MANDATORY for BARKAT
> SaaS services pe **18% GST** lagti hai — yeh lena zaroori hai

- **Kahan:** [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in)
- **Documents:** COI, PAN, Bank Statement, Office Address Proof
- **Time:** 3–7 working days
- **Cost:** ₹0 (completely free)
- **GSTIN milega** — isko apni invoices pe daalna hoga

---

#### 📌 Step 7: Razorpay Business Account Activate Karo
> BARKAT mein Razorpay integration hai — iske liye verified business account chahiye

- **Kahan:** [razorpay.com](https://razorpay.com)
- **Documents:** COI + GST Number + Bank Account
- **Time:** 2–5 days KYC verification
- **Cost:** ₹0 setup fee

| Fee Type | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Setup | ₹0 |
| Per transaction | 2% of order value |
| Payout | ₹5–₹15 per transfer |

---

#### 📌 Step 8: Trademark File Karo — "BARKAT" naam protect karo
> Koi aur tumhara naam use na kare

- **Kahan:** [ipindiaservices.gov.in](https://ipindiaservices.gov.in/trademarkefiling)
- **Class:** Class 42 (Software/SaaS services)
- **Time:** 18–24 months (but TM™ symbol turant use kar sakte ho)
- **Cost:**

| Entity Type | Govt Fee |
|-------------|----------|
| Pvt Ltd (Startup/MSME) | ₹4,500 per class |
| Pvt Ltd (Normal) | ₹9,000 per class |
| CA/Attorney fee | ₹3,000 – ₹8,000 |
